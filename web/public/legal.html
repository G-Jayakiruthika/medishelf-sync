Privacy notice

Last updated 16 November 2026. Written for India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

Who we are

AushadhBox is a family medicine cabinet app built by Jayakiruthika G. For this service we are the Data Fiduciary. To reach us or our Grievance Officer, use Privacy → Make a request inside the app; every request gets a reply within 30 days.

Children

Accounts are for adults (18+). A parent or guardian may add a child’s details after giving verifiable consent in the app (DPDP Act s.9). We do not track children or show them advertising.

Who receives data

Supabase (our database and sign-in provider) stores it on our behalf. Only people you invite into your household can see your household’s data. We never sell data, and the app has no advertising or third-party trackers.

Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), database rules that let each person reach only their own household, role-based permissions (owner, caregiver, viewer), and an activity log. Designed around the ISO/IEC 29100 privacy principles and ISO/IEC 27701 records of processing; this is a design aim, not a certification. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Rules require.

Your rights

In the app’s Privacy page you can download your data, correct it, erase it, withdraw consent, name a nominee, raise a grievance, or delete your account. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Terms of use

Last updated 16 November 2026.

1. Not medical advice

AushadhBox helps you keep track of medicines. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace your doctor or pharmacist. Reading checks use general adult ranges. In an emergency call 108 or 112.

2. Your account

You must be 18 or older. Keep your password private. You are responsible for what you add and for inviting only people you trust into your household.

3. Price

The family app is free. There are no hidden fees; anything paid in future will be shown with its price before you choose it.

4. Availability

We work to keep the service running but cannot promise it will never be interrupted. Keep paper prescriptions as the law requires.

5. Ending

You can delete your account at any time from the Privacy page. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
