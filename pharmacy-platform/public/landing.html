Built for how Indian pharmacies really work

Khata, not paper

Credit for regular customers, with limits, monthly reminders after harvest or salary day, and the same book visible to the customer in their app or by SMS. No more arguments about how much is owed.

UPI without a card machine

No printed QR needed. The app makes a UPI QR from your UPI ID or phone number with the exact bill amount. Cash, someone else's QR, points: every payment is recorded, and payments customers report wait for your confirmation.

Orders from everyone

App orders, SMS (AB shopcode dolo 650 2), a missed call that becomes a call-back task, phone orders and ASHA workers ordering for families. All in one list.

Never say "not available" again

Link with pharmacies near you. When you don't have a medicine, see who does, pass the order on, and earn the referral share. Move stock between your own shops before it expires.

Delivery that pays for itself

Your rider gets a route, the customer gives a 4-digit code on delivery, cash is recorded. Long trips are allowed only when worth it: enough margin, an essential medicine like insulin, other stops on the way, or the customer pays. Village pickup points for weekly rounds.

The family card

Scan a customer's AushadhBox card: with their permission you see their current medicines and allergies, check for dangerous combinations before handing over, and the medicines go into their app with dose reminders. They come back to you for refills.

Stock that stays in step

Batch and expiry for every strip, first-expiring sold first, never above MRP. Import from Marg, GoFrugal, Medeil or Tally, or install the sync agent on your billing computer.

Fridge you can prove

A ₹600 sensor in the insulin and vaccine fridge reports every 5 minutes and beeps when it's too warm or cold. 30-day log for inspections.

Rules built in

Prescriptions checked for Schedule H, counter-only for H1 and X, home delivery only inside your district, wholesale licence needed to sell stock to another shop. Every action logged with the staff member's name.

How it works

  1. Register with your drug licence. We check it, usually within one working day.
  2. Print your QR poster for the counter. Customers scan it to pay, order, see stock and their khata, or send an SMS with your shop code.
  3. Add your stock by import, the sync agent, or as it arrives. Customers see only "in stock / few left", never your counts or prices paid.
  4. Link with pharmacies nearby, add your staff and delivery person, set your delivery rules. Done.
