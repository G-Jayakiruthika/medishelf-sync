      How it works

      1. The family switches the card on in their AushadhBox app (Notebook → AushadhBox card). They choose: you may add medicines, and/or you may see their current medicines and allergies.
      2. You scan the QR or type the 12-letter code. You see only what they allowed; the name is shortened.
      3. Add what you give. Check safety first: duplicates, risky pairs and allergies are flagged before you hand over the medicines.
      4. Send. The medicines appear in their app with reminder times, the days of the course and the pack count, so the family gets refill alerts and asks you to keep them ready.

      Every look and every addition is shown to the family with your pharmacy's name. They can block a pharmacy at any time. Do not scan a card unless the customer shows it to you. AushadhBox does not replace your professional judgement.
